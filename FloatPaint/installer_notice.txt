FloatPaint (AERO-BRUSH) — Local Installation & Safety Notice
=============================================================

Thank you for installing FloatPaint!

What this software does:
- Launches a 100% local 2D/3D Air-Gesture Painting Studio in your browser
  (served only on localhost: http://127.0.0.1:8080 and ws://127.0.0.1:8765).
- Uses your webcam locally via OpenCV & MediaPipe ONLY while the app is open
  to detect hand landmarks in real time. No video or images are uploaded or
  stored remotely.
- Includes zero telemetry, zero ads, and zero background startup services.
- Installs per-user into your local AppData folder (no Administrator rights
  required) and includes a standard Windows Uninstaller.

Note on Windows SmartScreen:
If this installer is not yet signed with a commercial Authenticode certificate,
Windows SmartScreen may display an "Unknown publisher" notice on first run.
Click "More info" -> "Run anyway" if you downloaded this directly from the
author's official GitHub repository (github.com/BorceKhen/My-Personal-Works).
