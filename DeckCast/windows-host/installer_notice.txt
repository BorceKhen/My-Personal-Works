DeckCast Windows Studio — Local Installation & Safety Notice
===========================================================

Thank you for installing DeckCast!

What this software does:
- Streams your Windows screen to DeckCast Android controllers over your
  local Wi-Fi/hotspot using peer-to-peer WebRTC (zero internet servers).
- Lets you view & control Android DeckCast Hosts directly from Windows.
- Advertises and discovers peers locally via mDNS (_remotedisplay._tcp.)
  and hosts a local WebSocket signaling endpoint on port 9443 while active.
- Zero telemetry, zero cloud connections, and zero background startup tasks.
- Installs per-user into your local AppData folder (no Admin prompt needed).
